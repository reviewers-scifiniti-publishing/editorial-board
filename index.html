What board members do

  • Review 2 to 3 manuscripts a year in your field, and suggest reviewers for others.
  • Contribute or invite one manuscript a year.
  • Share the journal with colleagues, at conferences and on social media.
Term: 2 years, renewableNo fee to join

Read Scifiniti's guidelines for editors

What you receive

  • Free open access publication of your own research articles.
  • Free English editing and graphics enhancement when you publish in any Scifiniti journal.
  • Free English editing for one of your papers in any international journal.
  • Your profile on the journal's editorial board page.
  • A certificate of acknowledgement when you join.

Questions people ask

Is there a fee? No, joining the board is free.

How are members chosen? The Editor-in-Chief reviews each application against the board criteria: a PhD, a post at a university, research institute or company, and a publication record in the journal's field.

How long does it take? The editorial office writes to you with the decision within three weeks.

Who sees my CV? The editorial office and the Editor-in-Chief.

    How we use your information

    Scifiniti Publishing (MBZ City, Abu Dhabi, UAE) uses what you send to consider you for the editorial board of the journal. The editorial office and the Editor-in-Chief read it. It is stored in Scifiniti's Google Workspace.

    If you are appointed, your name, position, institution and country appear on the journal's editorial board page. Your photo and short bio appear only if you tick the box above.

    If you are not appointed, we keep your application for up to 2 years so the editors can consider you for later openings. You can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete it: write to info@scifiniti.com.
